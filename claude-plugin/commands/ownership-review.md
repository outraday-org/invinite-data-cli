# Review Ownership

Review ownership activity for `$ARGUMENTS` using Invinite Data.

1. Resolve the company identifier.
2. Fetch institutional holdings, institutional transactions, and insider trades for the requested period.
3. Identify the largest holders, notable new or exited positions, and meaningful insider acquisitions or dispositions.
4. Distinguish position value from share count and filing date from transaction date.
5. Present concise tables and call out reporting-lag limitations in institutional filings.
